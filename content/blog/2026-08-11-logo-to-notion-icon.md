# Logo to Notion Icon: Turn Your Logo Into a Square Icon

You're setting up a Notion workspace or a top-level page, and you want your logo as the icon instead of an emoji. Notion takes a **square image** and shows it small — in the sidebar, the workspace switcher, and at the top of the page.

There's no strict size requirement, so the only real job is a clean square that stays crisp when Notion shrinks it. A 256×256 or 512×512 square PNG covers everywhere the icon appears. Autocropper makes that from your logo, in your browser, with nothing uploaded to a server.

## What Notion needs

| Requirement | Value |
|---|---|
| Shape | Square (1:1) |
| Size | No strict minimum; 256×256 or 512×512 is plenty |
| Format | PNG (transparent works — see the catch below) |
| Where it shows | Sidebar, workspace switcher, page header — always small |

Notion displays the icon square, so upload a square: anything important near the edges won't get cut.

## Make it from your logo in three steps

1. **Drop your logo in.** The tool finds the mark and trims the dead space around it, so a logo with heavy padding fills the square instead of sitting tiny in the middle — which matters when Notion is already shrinking it.
2. **Take the 256 or 512 square.** Pick either from the size ladder. Each is downscaled with Lanczos-4 resampling, so it stays sharp at the small sizes Notion actually renders instead of going soft.
3. **Download, then upload to Notion.** In Notion, click the page or workspace icon, choose **Upload**, and drop the PNG in.

## The dark-mode catch

Autocropper outputs a **transparent** PNG. Notion has both a **light and a dark theme**, and a transparent icon takes on whatever background is behind it. A dark logo on transparency disappears in dark mode; a light logo disappears in light mode.

If your mark needs to read in both themes, put it on a **solid square** — your brand color, or a neutral that holds up light and dark — so it's visible either way. If you only ever use one theme, transparent is fine.

The tool works on logos and graphic marks on flat backgrounds, not photos, and it produces **squares only** — exactly right for a Notion icon, though not for a cover image or banner.

## FAQ

**What size should a Notion icon be?**
Notion doesn't publish a hard minimum — it accepts a square image and displays it small. A 256×256 or 512×512 square PNG is more than enough and stays crisp everywhere it appears.

**Can I use a transparent PNG as a Notion icon?**
Yes, but it may disappear in one theme: a dark logo vanishes in dark mode, a light logo in light mode. Put the mark on a solid background if it needs to read in both.

**Does Notion crop my icon?**
It shows the icon square. Upload a square so nothing near the edges gets cut off.

**Can I use my logo instead of an emoji?**
Yes — click the icon, choose Upload, and use a custom image. A square PNG of your logo is exactly what it expects.
